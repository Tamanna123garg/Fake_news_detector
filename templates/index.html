Fake News Detector

NLP decision support: TF-IDF text model + stylometric ensemble, with LIME-style explanations

Human review required. This tool scores wording and writing style. It does not verify facts and must not be used to flag political content without a human reviewer. See the model card limitations.

Short statements use the TF-IDF text model (trained on LIAR). Articles of 200+ words also use the stylometric ensemble (trained on dataset.csv); the two probabilities are averaged.

Model performance & limitations

  • Accuracy is modest (about 62-64% on the LIAR benchmark): wrong answers are common. Treat output as a prompt for a human fact-checker, never as a verdict.
  • The text model is trained on short English-language U.S. political statements (PolitiFact, 2007-2016) and will not transfer well to other topics, languages or years.
  • The style model is trained on a dataset whose rows 501-5000 are synthetic; its metrics describe that data, not real-world news.
  • Neither model checks facts. Satire, opinion, or sensational-but-true writing may be mis-flagged.
  • Political leaning is not a model input; it is used only to audit for disparate error rates.
